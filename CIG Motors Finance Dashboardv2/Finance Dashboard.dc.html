Management commentary

August 2026 · prepared by Group FP&A, reviewed by the Financial Controller

Revenue of ₦4.20B was ₦130m (3.0%) below budget. The shortfall is entirely in new vehicle units — 41 fewer than plan, concentrated in the GAC passenger range where two shipments arrived late in the month and could not be invoiced before period close. Aftersales was ahead of plan on both parts and labour.

Gross margin held at 30.0% against budget of 30.3%. Landed costs came in ₦10m under plan as the naira was stable through the month, offsetting a ₦45m overspend on customer rebates used to clear ageing GNI stock. Excluding those rebates, margin would have read 31.1%.

Operating expenses of ₦710m were ₦40m (6.0%) over budget, of which ₦25m is logistics: third-party haulage rates on the Lagos–Abuja lane rose in the month and two out-of-cycle deliveries were made at spot rates. Professional fees are ₦4m over on the interim audit fieldwork brought forward from September.

Cash closed at ₦0.82B, ₦260m up on the month, but the inflow is stock-funded rather than earnings-funded: a ₦170m inventory drawdown offset the ₦180m receivables build. Receivables are the single balance-sheet concern — DSO of 52 days against a 45-day policy, with ₦400m sitting in two public-sector accounts now past 60 days.
